Today, there are some interesting ideas, but none stand out as strong, standalone products. Most have medium confidence and face risks from larger companies. However, there are research leads worth exploring further.

## Buildable Ideas

### Local-First Password Manager
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** Users want more security and trust in password management.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Individuals and small teams concerned about password security and privacy.  
**How you'd win:** A local-first, open-source password manager that gives users control over their data.  
**First build:** Develop a basic version that allows users to manage passwords without cloud dependency.  
**Niche test:** Target individuals and small teams focused on security, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium; a big company could add local-first features to their existing tools.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could create a similar feature, reducing the need for a separate product.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://github.com/flythenimbus/bramble)
