## Research Leads
### Integrated Medical Coordination for Family Caregivers
**Category:** Health
**Problem:** Family caregivers are overwhelmed by the constant work of managing complex medical information, appointments, and medications for patients across many doctors and family members.
**Who'd pay:** Family caregivers who manage complex medical needs for a patient across multiple healthcare providers and family members.
**First build:** A mobile app allowing a primary caregiver to input and organize all patient medical information (diagnoses, allergies, provider contacts), schedule appointments with reminders for all involved parties, and manage medication schedules with alerts. It must also allow invited family members to view and contribute relevant information.
**Confidence:** HIGH. The biggest risk is the difficulty of getting comprehensive medical information from various healthcare providers and ensuring its accuracy, as well as user adoption by both caregivers and potentially healthcare teams.
**Sources:** [Web](https://thancguide.org/2025/08/the-journey/effectively-managing-medical-information-appointments), [Web](https://myhealthrex.com/health-records-for-caregivers)

### Cost-Effective, Anti-Bot Web Scraping API
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Reliably scraping modern websites is difficult and costly due to advanced anti-bot measures and the high resource consumption of traditional headless browser solutions.
**Who'd pay:** Developers and businesses needing to extract data at scale from modern, JavaScript-heavy websites for production workloads, especially those facing high costs and anti-bot challenges.
**First build:** A specialized API that reliably scrapes data from a specific category of anti-bot protected websites (e.g., e-commerce sites) at a demonstrably lower cost per page or data point than existing solutions.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM. The market is already crowded with many established players offering similar web scraping APIs and services, making customer acquisition and differentiation difficult.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://github.com/0xchasercat/draco/)

### Affordable AI Agent Observability with Full Traces and Cost Tracking
**Category:** Ai Agents
**Problem:** AI agent developers are frustrated by the lack of clear cost tracking, inadequate debugging tools that don't show full traces, and the high price of existing observability SaaS solutions.
**Who'd pay:** Developers building and deploying AI agent systems across multiple AI providers.
**First build:** A hosted service that captures agent logs and traces, visualizes full execution paths with costs per step, and provides a debugger for failed runs, focusing on a lower price point than existing enterprise solutions.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM. The market is already crowded with many existing tools offering similar features, making differentiation and market capture challenging.
**Sources:** [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/comments/1quf6iv/i_built_an_opensource_observability_tool_for_ai)

### Noreply.net Email Leak Prevention
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Companies are accidentally sending sensitive information to 'noreply.net' and similar domains. This happens because their email systems are set up incorrectly, leading to data leaks.
**Who'd pay:** IT security teams, compliance officers, or engineers who set up outbound email systems within companies, especially small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) that lack awareness or resources for complex security suites.
**First build:** A web-based tool where a company can input its domain and email sending configurations. The tool would scan for common misconfigurations that could route sensitive emails to 'noreply.net' and provide clear, step-by-step instructions to fix them.
**Confidence:** medium. Big email security providers like Barracuda, or even major email service providers like Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, could add a specific check or warning for this type of misconfiguration as a standard feature.
**Sources:** [Lobsters](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/08/a-researcher-bought-noreply-net-companies-started-sending-him-secrets/)

### Integrated Gym Management Software
**Category:** Fitness
**Problem:** Gym owners and personal trainers struggle with administrative tasks, inconsistent client scheduling, payment processing, and operational inefficiencies because they use manual processes or disconnected software for memberships, CRM, scheduling, and attendance.
**Who'd pay:** Gym owners and personal trainers, including those running fitness studios and martial arts academies.
**First build:** A simple web application that allows a gym owner to manage leads from their website, automatically create client profiles, schedule a class, and process a payment, all within one seamless workflow without manual data re-entry.
**Confidence:** low. The core problem of disconnected platforms and manual processes is already directly addressed by multiple established 'all-in-one' gym management software solutions, making it difficult for a new product to differentiate.
