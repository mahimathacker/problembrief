## Research Leads

### Difficulty in testing webhooks due to manual triggering and lack of real-time capture.
**Signal:** Developers are frustrated with manually triggering events to test webhooks, indicating a need for better integration testing tools.
**Why it is not ready:** Lack of clear buyer or market validation beyond individual developer frustration.
**Validate next:** Investigate if teams with complex webhook integrations would allocate budget for a dedicated testing tool.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://flurryport.dev/try)

### Lack of objective metrics for AI-generated code quality.
**Signal:** Developers want metrics to evaluate AI-generated code quality, which is currently missing from AI benchmarks.
**Why it is not ready:** No evidence of a specific buyer or budget owner willing to pay for such metrics.
**Validate next:** Determine if AI tool vendors or large development teams would invest in code quality benchmarking tools.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48488990)

### Lack of an ADR system makes large-scale refactoring difficult and frustrating.
**Signal:** Developers struggle with large-scale refactoring due to the absence of an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) system.
**Why it is not ready:** Unclear if this pain is widespread enough to justify a standalone product.
**Validate next:** Assess if engineering managers or tech leads in large organizations see ADR systems as a critical need.
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/pewdiepie-archdaemon/odysseus/issues/1998)
