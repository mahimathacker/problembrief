## TL;DR

Today’s strongest idea is **revenue-ranked site friction alerts for online stores**. Stores already lose a lot of money from slow pages, broken clicks, bad search, and checkout problems. A new tool can win by showing the exact buying problem that costs the most money right now.

## Buildable Ideas

### Revenue-ranked site friction alerts for online stores

**Category:** Ecommerce

**Problem:** Online stores lose buyers when pages are slow, clicks break, search fails, or checkout is confusing.

**What's already out there:** BigCommerce helps with online stores and abandoned carts, but pricing is unclear. Google Cloud Retail Search helps with product search, but pricing is unclear. Coveo helps with search and commerce experience, but pricing is unclear. Jotform can help with checkout forms, but pricing is unclear. Many tools exist here, which is a good sign that stores care about this problem.

**Who'd pay:** Ecommerce managers, growth teams, and web developers at online stores with enough daily traffic to see cart drops.

**How you'd win:** Do not build another broad analytics dashboard. Focus only on problems that stop people from buying. Rank each issue by cart value, shopper count, and checkout step. Send a clear fix ticket to the developer.

**First build:** Build a small script for one ecommerce platform. Track slow pages, error messages, dead clicks, rage clicks, failed searches, and checkout drop-offs. Show a daily list ranked by likely lost revenue. Include replay clips, URL, device, browser, cart value, and a suggested fix. Test it with 10 stores.

**Confidence:** High — the main risk is too many alerts. Teams will ignore it if it does not show what is costing money right now.
