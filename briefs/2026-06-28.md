## TL;DR

The strongest idea today is the simple salon admin tool. Many tools already exist, which is a good sign. But small salons still complain about hidden fees, hard setup, deposits, reminders, and daily admin work.

## Buildable Ideas

### Simple salon admin tool for small salons

**Category:** Small business

**Problem:** Small salon owners still do too much booking, deposit, reminder, payment, and stock work by hand.

**What's already out there:** Reservio has a free plan. GlossGenius, Vagaro, Mangomint, Booksy Biz, Zenoti, Phorest, and EnvisionNow are also in this market. Some tools for solo users are reported around free to $50/month. Many tools already there means people want this.

**Who'd pay:** Small salon owners with 1 to 10 staff.

**How you'd win:** Make it very easy on a phone. Use clear flat pricing. No hidden fees. Focus on deposits, reminders, simple stock tasks, and a daily to-do list.

**First build:** Build online booking, deposit collection, automatic reminders, client notes, simple stock counts, and a daily task list. Test with 10 salon owners who use spreadsheets, messages, or a tool they dislike.

**Confidence:** Medium — salons may hate their current tool but still not switch because moving bookings, clients, and payments is painful.

### Retail Shipment Visibility for CPG Brands

**Category:** Vertical saas

**Problem:** CPG brands lose money when shipments are late, damaged, hard to track, or miss retail delivery rules.

**What's already out there:** Hylios gives CPG supply chain visibility guidance, pricing unclear. RJW is named as a logistics visibility and coordination provider, pricing unclear. Shopify Enterprise supports commerce and supply chain work, pricing unclear. This market already has players, so the pain is real.

**Who'd pay:** Logistics, supply chain, or operations leaders at small and mid-size CPG brands that ship to retail distribution centers.

**How you'd win:** Focus only on CPG shipments going into retail distribution centers. Make carrier follow-up, delivery appointments, late shipment alerts, and compliance records simple for small teams.

**First build:** Build a simple shipment tracker. Start with email upload, CSV upload, and manual entry. Track delivery date, appointment time, retailer rules, late loads, missing appointments, damage claims, and weekly carrier reports.

**Confidence:** Medium — clean shipment data may be hard to get from carriers, 3PLs, and retail portals.

**Sources:** [Web](https://ziplinelogistics.com/blog/logistics-solutions-guide), [Web](https://bluegistics.com/7-pain-points-logistics-transportation-before-tms)

### Plain-English Monthly Updates for Accounting Clients

**Category:** Small business

**Problem:** Small business owners often only hear from their accountant at tax time, so they miss problems during the year.

**What's already out there:** Accounting firms already sell year-round advice, tax planning, cash flow help, and compliance help. Basic small business accounting services are listed around $1,000 to $5,000 per year. Specific software pricing is unclear. The existing spend is a good sign.

**Who'd pay:** Small accounting firms and bookkeepers who serve many small business clients.

**How you'd win:** Do not replace accountants. Help them send clear monthly client messages faster. The accountant reviews and approves each message before it goes out.

**First build:** Build a tool that takes CSV uploads or manual numbers from bookkeeping reports. It finds 3 to 5 issues, like cash flow risk, high expenses, missing tax payments, or deadlines. Then it writes a short plain-English email for the accountant to review and send.

**Confidence:** Medium — accountants may like the idea but not change their normal work unless it clearly saves time.
