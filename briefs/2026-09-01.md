## Research Leads
### Cash Flow Stability for Content Creators
**Category:** Marketing Creator Agencies
**Problem:** Content creators and freelancers frequently face delayed or unpaid invoices from brands and agencies, severely impacting their cash flow and business operations.
**Who'd pay:** Content creators and freelancers who work with brands and agencies.
**First build:** An automated system that integrates with existing invoicing tools (or offers basic invoicing) to track payment status, send escalating, personalized reminders, and provide a clear process for dispute resolution or a simple 'get paid now' option for verified invoices.
**Confidence:** medium. Big invoicing platforms like Stripe or QuickBooks could add more robust, automated late payment follow-up and dispute resolution features, or a large financial service could offer invoice factoring specifically for this niche.

### Niche Warm Lead Generation for Small Businesses
**Category:** Marketing Creator Agencies
**Problem:** Small businesses, SaaS founders, and e-commerce stores struggle to find new customers because cold outreach has very low response rates. They need better ways to get warm leads.
**Who'd pay:** Small businesses, SaaS founders, and e-commerce stores who need to acquire customers, specifically those looking for highly targeted and personalized outreach for a niche market.
**First build:** A tool that identifies potential customers for a single, very specific niche (e.g., 'newly funded SaaS startups in the HR tech space') based on public signals and provides highly personalized outreach templates and verified contact information.
**Confidence:** medium. The niche chosen might be too broad, making it hard to differentiate from existing generic cold email tools. Also, getting reliable data sources for truly 'warm' leads for many niches can be difficult to maintain.
