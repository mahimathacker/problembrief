## Research Leads
### AI Agent Long-Run Persistence
**Category:** Ai Agents
**Problem:** AI agents doing long tasks are stopped by short usage limits, causing them to lose their work and progress.
**Who'd pay:** Developers building and running long-duration AI agents.
**First build:** A tool that saves the state of a running AI agent before a usage limit hits and automatically resumes it in a new session.
**Confidence:** low. A big AI platform like OpenAI could add native persistence or longer usage windows for their agents, making a separate product unnecessary.
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/openai/codex/issues/40905)
