## Research Leads

### Unified Outbound Sales Management
**Category:** Small business  
**Problem:** Sales professionals struggle to manage their outbound sales activities across multiple platforms.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Sales professionals managing outbound activities.  
**How you'd win:** A dedicated platform that consolidates all outbound sales activities into one interface.  
**First build:** Build a simple dashboard that integrates with popular sales platforms.  
**Niche test:** Sales teams in small businesses, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is operational work, niche score 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium, could be absorbed by a big sales tool like Salesforce.  
**Confidence:** Medium, a big company could create a similar feature easily.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://boilerroom.ai)
