### Unified Medical Records for Caregivers
**Category:** Health
**Problem:** Patients and caregivers struggle to gather, organize, and share medical records from many different doctors. They face slow requests, confusing information, and too many online portals and passwords, which causes delays and risks in care.
**Who'd pay:** Caregivers, especially those managing complex chronic conditions or senior care for their loved ones.
**First build:** A tool that helps a caregiver list all providers, then automates the record request process (e.g., generating forms, tracking status, sending reminders) and consolidates received documents into a single, searchable timeline.
**Confidence:** HIGH. The biggest risk is the complexity of integrating with many different health systems and portals, and navigating strict privacy regulations like HIPAA.
**Sources:** [Web](https://baycrestfoundation.org/articles/young-caregivers/dementia-caregivers-guide-to-managing-healthcare-appointments-medical-records), [Web](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Am7rVKHXSM), [Web](https://reddicareconnections.com/keeping-records-and-managing-medications-for-the-elderly-essential-practices-for-caregivers), [Web](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DObXS-tEeVo?hl=en), [Web](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10464417)

### Unified Linux Software Packaging
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Developers find it very frustrating and complex to package their software for the many different Linux distributions, which each need their own formats and methods.
**Who'd pay:** Independent software vendors (ISVs) and open-source project maintainers who need to distribute their applications across many Linux distributions.
**First build:** A command-line tool that takes a compiled binary and configuration, then generates valid Snap, Flatpak, and AppImage packages with minimal setup.
**Confidence:** HIGH. Existing universal formats like Snap and Flatpak are already widely adopted and are continuously improving, making it hard for a new tool to gain traction unless it offers a significantly better, more unified, or easier experience.
**Sources:** [Lobsters](https://getfresh.dev/docs/blog/packaging-for-linux/)

### Integrated Salon Management Software
**Category:** Fashion Beauty
**Problem:** Salon owners face chaos, lost revenue, and staff burnout because manual appointment management and generic booking tools lack essential features like 24/7 online booking, automated reminders, commission structures, stylist-specific rules, and deposit collection.
**Who'd pay:** Independent salon owners who are struggling with operational chaos and revenue loss due to disconnected or inadequate management tools.
**First build:** A single platform offering 24/7 online booking, automated reminders, deposit collection, and integrated POS with basic commission tracking and inventory management for a single salon location.
**Confidence:** MEDIUM. The market is crowded with many established, specialized salon software solutions like Zenoti, Vagaro, Boulevard, and Mangomint, which already offer many of the core features. Differentiation will be challenging.
**Sources:** [Web](https://envisionnow.com/resources/blog/best-salon-software), [Web](https://salontarget.com/how-salon-booking-software-simplifies-scheduling), [Web](https://www.daysmart.com/salon/blog/best-salon-management-software)

### Unified Cross-Platform Subscription Management for Desktop Apps
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Developers struggle to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions across mobile, web, and desktop operating systems. This leads to manual setup, limited user experience, and users being restricted to their subscription on a single platform.
**Who'd pay:** Developers building applications with subscription models that target mobile, web, and desktop operating systems (MacOS, Windows, Linux).
**First build:** A server-side API and SDKs for common desktop frameworks (e.g., Electron, native C++/C#) that allow developers to define subscription products once and manage user entitlements universally across Apple App Store, Google Play, Stripe (for web), and direct desktop purchases, ensuring a user's subscription works on any platform they use.
**Confidence:** medium. Existing subscription management tools like RevenueCat, Apphud, or Paddle could extend their offerings to cover desktop operating systems more comprehensively, or the demand for a truly unified solution across all desktop OSes (including Linux) might be too niche to sustain a standalone product.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49286271)

### Pre-Visit Prep for Chronic Patients
**Category:** Health
**Problem:** Patients with chronic illnesses or those seeing many specialists struggle to prepare for doctor visits. They forget details, cannot track symptoms and medications well, and find appointments rushed and frustrating.
**Who'd pay:** Patients with chronic illnesses who see multiple specialists.
**First build:** A mobile app that allows patients to log symptoms, medications, and questions, then generates a simple, printable summary report for their doctor, highlighting key changes and concerns since the last visit.
**Confidence:** medium. Existing patient-facing apps like Bearable or Redi.Health could add more robust pre-visit reporting and multi-specialist coordination features, making a new product less necessary.
