## TL;DR
Today, there are several buildable ideas, but the strongest one is the "Autosave for Story Drafts." It addresses a common problem for writers and content creators who lose their work. This tool can fill a gap in the market and has a clear path to success.

## Buildable Ideas

### Autosave for Story Drafts
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** Users lose unsaved story drafts after refreshing the page.  
**What's already out there:** Instagram drafts can be lost due to app updates; Threads users report losing drafts after refreshing; No built-in solutions to recover lost drafts on major platforms.  
**Who'd pay:** Writers and content creators who frequently draft stories or posts online.  
**How you'd win:** A dedicated autosave tool that specifically targets the problem of lost drafts across multiple platforms.  
**First build:** Build a simple browser extension that autosaves text input in real-time for popular writing platforms.  
**Niche test:** Target writers and content creators, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium risk; Google or Microsoft could add similar features.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily compete by adding autosave features.  
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/ronisarkarexe/story-spark-ai/issues/4057)
