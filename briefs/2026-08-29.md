## TL;DR
Today's best idea is a tool to help music creators claim their missing royalties. Hundreds of millions of dollars are unclaimed, and a simple guide could help independent artists get paid.

## Buildable Ideas

### Unclaimed Music Royalty Assistant
**Category:** Marketing creator agencies
**Problem:** Music creators lose money because they don't know how to register their songs or fix song data.
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.
**Who'd pay:** Independent music creators, songwriters, and small music publishers.
**How you'd win:** A simple tool that guides creators to understand, register, and claim their royalties. It would automate much of the hard work.
**First build:** A website that helps creators check their song registration with The MLC. It would find common data problems and show how to fix them and claim money.
**Niche test:** This tool is for independent music creators, songwriters, and small music publishers. 4/5
**Boring test:** This is about fixing data and following rules to get paid. It's very unsexy but important. 5/5
**Big-company risk:** medium. Likely absorber: Major music distributors or rights management organizations.
**Confidence:** medium. The biggest risk is that big music companies might already offer this service.
