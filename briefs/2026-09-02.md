## Research Leads
### Faster Pyodide/Jupyterlite Package Loading
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Developers using Pyodide and Jupyterlite face extremely long initial load times (up to 60 seconds) when using large Python packages like OpenCV, NumPy, and SciPy. This happens even with local caching, mainly due to WebAssembly compile time.
**Who'd pay:** Developers building browser-based Python applications or interactive notebooks using Pyodide/Jupyterlite, especially those who rely on large scientific or data analysis packages.
**First build:** A service that hosts and serves pre-compiled, optimized WASM versions of a few key large packages (e.g., NumPy, SciPy) for Pyodide, demonstrating a measurable reduction in initial load times for a simple application.
**Confidence:** HIGH. The technical challenge of consistently and significantly reducing WASM compile times across various packages and browser environments might be harder than anticipated, or the underlying WASM technology could evolve in a way that makes a third-party solution less necessary.
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/pyodide/pyodide/issues/6418)

### Admin Tool for Nano/Micro Creators
**Category:** Marketing Creator Agencies
**Problem:** Nano and micro creators are overwhelmed by the administrative burden of managing collaborations. This includes negotiations, contract reviews, compliance, approvals, billing, and analytics, often without support from managers or agents.
**Who'd pay:** Nano and micro creators who treat content creation as a business and manage their own collaborations.
**First build:** A web app where a nano/micro creator can generate a basic, compliant one-page contract, track payment status for collaborations, and log deliverables.
**Confidence:** HIGH. Nano and micro creators might not be willing to pay enough for a dedicated tool, preferring free templates or simple manual methods, given their small deal sizes ($50-$300 per video for nano influencers).
**Sources:** [Web](https://www.pragencyone.co.uk/uncategorized/bad-influencer-marketing-collabs-and-how-brands-can-fix-them)

### Patient & Caregiver Health Data Hub
**Category:** Health
**Problem:** Patients and caregivers struggle to track, update, and combine medical information from many different sources like records, appointments, symptoms, and wearable devices. This is especially hard for chronic illnesses and when seeing multiple doctors.
**Who'd pay:** Caregivers of patients with complex chronic illnesses who see many different doctors.
**First build:** A simple tool that lets caregivers connect to 2-3 common patient portals, manually add prescriptions and appointments, and upload basic data from one type of wearable device. It would then show a clear summary of all this information for a specific chronic condition.
**Confidence:** medium. It will be very hard to get data from all the different medical record systems and other sources due to technical and privacy rules. Also, existing patient-focused tools like wisepatientAI are already working on this problem.
