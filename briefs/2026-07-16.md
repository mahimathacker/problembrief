## TL;DR
Today, the strongest buildable idea is the **Salon Booking and Reminder System**. Many small salons need better tools to manage bookings and reduce no-shows. This idea has a clear market demand and a simple solution to build.

## Buildable Ideas

### Salon Booking and Reminder System
**Category:** Small business  
**Problem:** Salons without online booking and automated reminders lose revenue.  
**What's already out there:** Reservio (free plan), Vagaro, EnvisionNow, SimplerBook (free booking system).  
**Who'd pay:** Small to medium salon owners.  
**How you'd win:** Provide a simple, affordable booking and reminder system specifically for small salons.  
**First build:** A basic online booking system with automated reminders for salons.  
**Niche test:** Small salon owners managing fewer than 50 clients, 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium; Microsoft or Google could add similar features.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily enter this space.  
**Sources:** [Web](https://salontarget.com/affordable-salon-software-solutions-for-small-businesses)

### Preventing Data Loss from Accidental Page Reloads
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** Accidental page reloads can cause users to lose unsaved posts.  
**What's already out there:** Browsers can prevent accidental reloads with JavaScript's beforeunload event, WordPress and Chrome have implemented warnings.  
**Who'd pay:** Developers and content creators who frequently compose online posts.  
**How you'd win:** A dedicated tool that provides a robust solution to prevent data loss from accidental reloads.  
**First build:** A simple browser extension that alerts users before they reload a page with unsaved changes.  
**Niche test:** Content creators and developers, 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is somewhat unsexy operational work, 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium; Google or Microsoft could add similar features.  
**Confidence:** Medium; existing companies could easily replicate this.  
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/bluesky-social/social-app/issues/11057)

### Efficient Motion Data Storage for BIDS
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** The plain-text TSV format for motion data in BIDS leads to large file sizes.  
**What's already out there:** EEG2BIDS, sovabids, FieldTrip toolbox.  
**Who'd pay:** Researchers and developers working with motion capture data.  
**How you'd win:** A specialized tool that compresses and optimizes motion data for BIDS.  
**First build:** A prototype that converts existing TSV files to a more efficient binary format.  
**Niche test:** Researchers in motion capture, 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is somewhat unsexy operational work, 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium; Google or Microsoft could integrate similar features.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily enter this space.  
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/bids-standard/bids-specification/issues/2463)
