## Research Leads
### Self-Serve SaaS Finder
**Category:** Marketing Creator Agencies
**Problem:** Marketers and small businesses struggle to find SaaS tools with transparent, self-serve, usage-based pricing, as many companies switch to sales-led models and annual commitments.
**Who'd pay:** Marketers and small business owners.
**First build:** A website listing SaaS tools that offer clear, self-serve, usage-based pricing, with a simple filter for categories.
**Confidence:** medium. Difficulty in continuously verifying and updating pricing models for many SaaS products.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49455036)

### Privacy-Friendly, Modern Web Analytics
**Category:** Marketing Creator Agencies
**Problem:** Small website owners, creators, and marketers need analytics that are privacy-friendly and lightweight, but still offer modern features like real-time visitor journeys, funnels, and revenue attribution, without using cookies or banners.
**Who'd pay:** Small website owners, creators, and marketers.
**First build:** A lightweight script for website integration, a dashboard showing real-time visitor journeys, basic funnels, and source attribution, all without using cookies or requiring consent banners.
**Confidence:** medium. The market might already be served by similar privacy-focused analytics tools not identified in the research, or the target buyer may not be willing to pay for a separate tool when free (less private) options exist.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://getopen.so)
