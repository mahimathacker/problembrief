## Research Leads
### Production Debugging for AI-Generated Code
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Engineers struggle with slow and painful production debugging, especially for AI-written code, because existing logs are often insufficient. This leads to costly analysis loops or repeated, time-consuming redeployments to add more logging.
**Who'd pay:** Engineers and developers who work with or maintain AI-generated code in production environments.
**First build:** A tool that integrates with AI code generation pipelines to automatically inject more detailed, context-rich logging, or allows engineers to dynamically add temporary logging to AI-generated code in production without requiring a full redeployment.
**Confidence:** HIGH. Existing general-purpose observability platforms could extend their offerings to specifically address the unique debugging challenges of AI-generated code, potentially absorbing this workflow.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://www.hyperprobe.co)

### Multi-Channel Order & Inventory Sync for Social Sellers
**Category:** Small Business
**Problem:** Small businesses face significant chaos and operational breakdowns due to manual order entry and disconnected systems for managing sales and inventory across various channels, and are willing to pay for tools to save time on critical operations like shipping, inventory management, and order syncing.
**Who'd pay:** Small e-commerce businesses that sell across multiple channels, including social media platforms like WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook, in addition to traditional platforms like Shopify or Amazon.
**First build:** A tool that automatically pulls orders from WhatsApp/Instagram DMs (via integrations or simple parsing), syncs inventory with Shopify/Amazon, and generates shipping labels for these combined orders.
**Confidence:** HIGH. The technical complexity and reliability of integrating with social media platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs) for order extraction, as these platforms are not primarily designed for e-commerce APIs in the same way Shopify is.

### AI Agent Cost and Reliability Management
**Category:** Ai Agents
**Problem:** AI agents often waste significant tokens and go off-rails, failing to complete tasks and leading to high costs and stalled projects. This includes excessive re-reads and unasked-for elaborate processes.
**Who'd pay:** Teams and developers building and deploying AI agents in production environments.
**First build:** A monitoring dashboard that tracks token usage per agent, identifies runaway loops, and allows users to set cost caps or behavioral constraints for their AI agents.
**Confidence:** HIGH. Big cloud providers or LLM platforms could integrate more sophisticated agent orchestration and cost management features directly into their offerings, making a standalone product less necessary.
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/76987)

### Private CRM for Real Estate Agents
**Category:** Real Estate
**Problem:** Real estate agents are worried about losing control and ownership of their client data when using third-party CRMs. This concern is especially high when marketing companies store leads in those CRMs, making agents consider using private or duplicate CRMs to protect their 'digital equity'.
**Who'd pay:** Independent real estate agents or small real estate agencies.
**First build:** A simple CRM that allows agents to import contacts, track interactions, and offers a one-click, complete data export in a universal format (e.g., CSV, JSON) with clear terms of service guaranteeing data ownership.
**Confidence:** medium. Existing, established real estate CRMs could improve their data ownership features and terms, or agents might not value 'full data ownership' enough to switch from feature-rich, integrated platforms.
**Sources:** [Web](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DQog2bUERg8?hl=en)

### Reliable AI Agent Evaluation and Debugging
**Category:** Ai Agents
**Problem:** Developers struggle to build reliable AI agents because agents are unpredictable. They lack good ways to test them, manage their context, and see what's happening inside them. This causes agents to fail in unexpected ways and makes it hard to understand user interactions.
**Who'd pay:** Developers and engineering teams building and deploying multi-step AI agents in enterprise production environments.
**First build:** A tool that allows developers to define custom evaluation metrics for non-deterministic AI agent outputs, run these evaluations against historical agent traces, and visualize reasoning paths and failure points. It should also offer replay capabilities for specific agent runs to aid debugging.
**Confidence:** medium. LangChain, with its existing LangSmith product, or other major AI framework providers (like OpenAI, Google) could significantly improve their built-in evaluation and debugging tools to cover the exact workflow, reducing the need for a standalone product.
