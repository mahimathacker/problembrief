## TL;DR
Today, the strongest buildable idea is the "Automated Booking for Salons." Many salon owners need better booking tools to reduce missed appointments. This idea has a clear market demand and a simple solution that can help small salons.

## Buildable Ideas

### Automated Booking for Salons
**Category:** Fashion beauty  
**Problem:** Salon owners lose money from missed appointments due to poor booking systems.  
**What's already out there:** Reservio (free plan), GlossGenius, Vagaro, Mangomint  
**Who'd pay:** Salon owners  
**How you'd win:** Focus on small-to-medium salons needing affordable, easy-to-use booking solutions.  
**First build:** A simple automated booking and follow-up tool for salons.  
**Niche test:** Small-to-medium salons, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 5/5.
