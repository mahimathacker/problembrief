## TL;DR

Today's strongest idea is a "Structured Patient Handoff Communication Tool" for healthcare. It aims to reduce errors in patient handoffs by integrating structured communication protocols with existing electronic health record (EHR) systems. This is important due to the increased focus on patient safety.

## Buildable Ideas

### Structured Patient Handoff Communication Tool
**Category:** Health  
**Problem:** Manual patient handoffs are error-prone and risky.  
**What people do today:** Healthcare staff manage handoffs manually with limited tools.  
**Job software could take over:** Manage patient handoffs with structured, clear communication between providers.  
**What still needs a human:** Interpreting patient data and making clinical decisions.  
**What's already out there:** I-PASS and SBAR frameworks, Dialog Health's HIPAA-compliant texting, Prevent Handoff Communication Errors (~$49/mo).  
**Who'd pay:** Healthcare administrators.  
**How you'd win:** Integrate structured communication protocols with existing EHR systems.  
**First build:** A digital tool that integrates with EHR systems for structured handoffs.  
**Niche test:** Healthcare administrators in hospitals, 4/5.  
**Boring test:** It's operational and unsexy, 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium, existing EHR systems could add similar features.  
**Why now:** Increased focus on patient safety and reducing medical errors.  
**Confidence:** Medium, biggest risk is EHR systems adding similar features.  
**Sources:** [Web](https://www.medicalmutual.com/risk/resources/handoff-communication/dff09570-27c5-420d-b0b2-bc92bf0157da)
