## TL;DR
Today, the strongest buildable idea is the **Junior Developer Mentorship Platform**. There is a clear need for mentorship for new developers, and no strong competitors exist. This could help bridge the gap in training for junior developers.

## Buildable Ideas

### Junior Developer Mentorship Platform
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** New developers lack mentorship and training.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Junior developers and companies wanting to train them.  
**How you'd win:** Focused mentorship programs tailored for junior developers.  
**First build:** Create a platform connecting junior developers with experienced mentors for guidance and training.  
**Niche test:** Junior developers looking for mentorship, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is operational work that helps new developers, boring score 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium risk; a big company could add mentorship features to their tools.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily enter this space.  
**Sources:** [Dev.to](https://dev.to/nazar-boyko/the-junior-developer-pipeline-is-broken-and-ai-broke-it-1aai)
