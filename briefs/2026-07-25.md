## TL;DR
Today, there are some buildable ideas, but they all have medium confidence. The strongest idea is the "Recreational Facility Management System." It targets small businesses that need affordable tools to manage facilities like bowling alleys. This market is underserved, making it a good opportunity.

## Buildable Ideas

### Recreational Facility Management System
**Category:** Small business  
**Problem:** Small businesses struggle with high costs and outdated systems for managing recreational facilities like bowling alleys.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Small businesses operating recreational facilities.  
**How you'd win:** Offer affordable, modern management tools tailored for small recreational facilities.  
**First build:** A simple score keeping app for bowling alleys that integrates with existing systems.  
**Niche test:** Small recreational facility owners, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium risk; a big company could create a similar tool as a feature in their existing management software.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily enter this space.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48968606)
