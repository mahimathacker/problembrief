## Research Leads
### Automated Building Plan and Permit Compliance
**Category:** Real Estate
**Problem:** Homeowners and contractors face project delays and rejections because they hand-draw building plans and do not understand local permit rules.
**Who'd pay:** Small to medium-sized general contractors and homeowners doing renovation projects.
**First build:** A tool that generates a simple, compliant plan for a common project type (like a deck or small addition) for one or two specific municipalities.
**Confidence:** medium. The biggest risk is the high complexity and constant changes in local building codes and permit requirements across many different municipalities. Keeping this data accurate and up-to-date is a huge challenge.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://myhomecat.com)

### Duplicate Issue Finder for Dev Teams
**Category:** Devtools
**Problem:** Development teams waste time checking for duplicate bug reports across different tools like GitHub and Discord, leading to fragmented knowledge and repeated effort.
**Who'd pay:** Small to medium-sized development teams that use GitHub for issue tracking and Discord for real-time communication and initial bug reporting.
**First build:** A bot that monitors Discord channels for bug reports, extracts key information, and then searches existing GitHub issues for similar text, alerting the team to potential duplicates.
**Confidence:** medium. The problem might not be painful enough or widespread enough for teams to pay for a dedicated tool, or a simple integration from GitHub or Discord could address it.
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49078625)
