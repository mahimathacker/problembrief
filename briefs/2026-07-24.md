## Research Leads

### Trailing Whitespace Fixer for Docker
**Category:** Devtools  
**Problem:** Trailing whitespace in .env.example files causes deployment failures.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Developers using Docker for deployment.  
**How you'd win:** A simple tool that automatically detects and removes trailing whitespace before deployment.  
**First build:** A command-line tool that scans .env.example files and removes any trailing whitespace.  
**Niche test:** Developers working with Docker, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium; a big company could add this feature to existing CI/CD tools.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily take this idea.  
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/itzzavdhesh/VoiceForge/issues/748)
