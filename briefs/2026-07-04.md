## TL;DR

The strongest idea today is **Hybrid BLE Test Lab**. The pain is clear, the buyer is clear, and the first build can be small: Android, one BLE dongle, and scripted fake devices.

**Water visibility API** is also buildable, but trust is the hard part. If the forecast is wrong often, dive operators will not use it.

## Buildable Ideas

### Hybrid BLE Test Lab

**Category:** Mobile  
**Problem:** BLE app teams need to test real Bluetooth behavior and fake devices in the same test flow.  
**What's already out there:** TestBot can mock BLE peripherals, pricing unclear. BleuIO is a BLE simulator, pricing unclear. Native phone scanning exists, but it does not add fake devices. Mock test setups exist, but often avoid the real Bluetooth stack. Many tools here is a good sign. It means teams need help.  
**Who'd pay:** QA leads and mobile engineers at teams building apps for wearables, health devices, cars, smart homes, or sensors.  
**How you'd win:** Focus only on BLE app release testing. Let a real phone scan normally, while fake scripted BLE devices appear like real ones.  
**First build:** Desktop test runner for Android with one BLE dongle. Support fake peripherals, BLE services, reads, writes, notifications, disconnects, weak signal, and bad data. Let real BLE devices stay visible too.  
**Confidence:** medium — The big risk is that TestBot, BleuIO, Apple, or Google add this same mixed real-and-fake test flow.  
**Sources:** [GitHub](https://github.com/nordicsemi/Kotlin-BLE-Library/issues/320)

### Water visibility API for dive operators

**Category:** Vertical SaaS  
**Problem:** Dive operators need a simple answer: will the water be clear enough today?  
**What's already out there:** Viz App has diver reports, pricing unclear. Deep Dive App shows satellite water clarity for lakes, pricing unclear. DiveSight has forecasts and is reported to start at $10/month. TCarta has satellite water quality data, pricing unclear. NOAA DIVER gives scientific data, not a simple dive tool. Submerge, Vis.report, and Willy Weather are also used, pricing unclear. Many tools here is a good sign. People already look for this answer.  
**Who'd pay:** Dive shops, spearfishing guides, snorkel tour operators, and charter boats.  
**How you'd win:** Start with one local area. Give a simple visibility score, confidence level, and a shareable customer link. Mix satellite data, weather, surf, and real diver reports.  
**First build:** Pick one area, like Southern California or Florida springs. For each site, show visibility range, last update, confidence, and reason. Add a small API for operators.  
**Confidence:** medium — The big risk is trust. Water visibility changes fast, and bad forecasts will push users back to local groups and reports.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://submarius.com)

### Fintech Scale Readiness Audit

**Category:** Fintech  
**Problem:** Fintech startups find uptime, cloud cost, and compliance problems too late.  
**What's already out there:** CloudGateway writes about fintech infrastructure pain and offers guidance, pricing unclear. Concentrix offers compliance help, pricing unclear. Zethic writes about backend scale problems, pricing unclear. Cloud finance tools exist, but no clear simple product or monthly price was found.  
**Who'd pay:** CTOs and engineering leaders at seed to Series B fintech startups before funding or an enterprise deal.  
**How you'd win:** Start as a fast paid audit, not a big tool. Give a plain-English report on what may break, what may cost too much, and what may scare buyers.  
**First build:** Connect to cloud billing, uptime logs, and a simple compliance checklist. Return the top 10 risks before a funding round or big customer deal. Charge for the audit first.  
**Confidence:** low — This may stay a service, or become a checklist inside cloud and compliance tools.  
**Sources:** [Web](https://www.cloudgateway.co.uk/knowledge-centre/articles/fintech-hidden-infrastructure-pain-points-that-drain-capital)

### CRM Data Clean-Up for Mid-Market Sales Teams

**Category:** Marketing  
**Problem:** Bad CRM data makes sales reports hard to trust and wastes rep time.  
**What's already out there:** Apollo writes about stale CRM data, pricing unclear. Hints.so writes about bad CRM data, pricing unclear. Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics can add rules and data checks, pricing unclear. PipelineCRM lists many CRM tools, pricing unclear. A crowded market is a good sign. Sales teams already spend money here.  
**Who'd pay:** Sales ops leaders at mid-market B2B sales teams.  
**How you'd win:** Do not sell a broad AI CRM. Start with weekly clean-up tasks for reps and clean pipeline reports for sales ops.  
**First build:** Salesforce or Dynamics add-on. Find missing fields, stale contacts, duplicate records, and deals with weak next steps. Send one task list to reps and one summary to sales ops.  
**Confidence:** low — Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics, or Apollo could add enough of this inside tools teams already use.  
**Sources:** [Web](https://cbtw.tech/insights/crm-usage-mistakes-how-to-fix-them), [Web](https://www.hq-digital.com/blog/the-struggle-is-real-4-common-pains-of-sales-teams-how-to-solve-them)

### Guix Substitute and Channel Watcher

**Category:** Security  
**Problem:** Guix users may install binary substitutes or channel updates that they did not build themselves. If attacked, they may get bad code.  
**What's already out there:** Guix has `guix substitute` and `guix pull`, pricing unclear. npm and pnpm have supply chain controls, pricing unclear. Debian 14 is reported to require reproducible builds. Flox is related to Nix-style workflows, pricing unclear. Minimus lists supply chain tools, pricing unclear. No clear direct Guix monitor was found.  
**Who'd pay:** Small security-heavy teams using Guix or Nix in build systems, research labs, crypto projects, or developer machines.  
**How you'd win:** Start with Guix only. Make pre-install warnings, channel change alerts, trusted substitute server rules, and audit logs.  
**First build:** Local command line tool for Guix. Watch channels, substitutes, signing keys, and hash mismatches. Warn before install or pull. Add simple rules like “wait 3 days before accepting a new substitute.”  
**Confidence:** low — This may become a normal feature inside Guix or Nix.  
**Sources:** [Lobsters](https://guix.gnu.org/en/blog/2026/guix-substitute-pull-vulnerabilities/)

### Practice Packs for Autistic and ADHD Teens

**Category:** Healthtech  
**Problem:** Neurodivergent teens often need calm practice before new social situations.  
**What's already out there:** Social Cipher is a game-based learning tool, starting at $25/month. Life Skills Advocate lists tools and resources, pricing unclear. NeuroPrism is a community, pricing unclear. Social skills courses, workshops, and books exist, pricing unclear. ChatGPT and Gemini can make social stories and role-play, pricing varies by plan. Many options here is a good sign. Families are already looking for help.  
**Who'd pay:** Parents of autistic or ADHD teens who want home practice before real events.  
**How you'd win:** Do fixed practice packs, not open-ended chat. Make it safe, concrete, parent-led, and usable in 10 minutes.  
**First build:** Web app with 20 practice packs. Start with airport visit, first therapy visit, group project, birthday party, texting a friend, and asking a teacher for help. Each pack has a short story, body language examples, a calm chat practice, and parent notes.  
**Confidence:** low — ChatGPT, Gemini, or school tools can do much of this if the product is only AI role-play.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://spotthecue.com/)
