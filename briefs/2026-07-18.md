## TL;DR
Today, there are several buildable ideas, but the strongest one is the **Timely Payment Solutions for Content Creators**. Many content creators struggle with getting paid on time, and a focused invoicing tool could help them manage payments better. This market shows strong demand, and existing solutions are limited.
