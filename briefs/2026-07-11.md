## TL;DR
Today, there are some buildable ideas, but they all have medium confidence. The strongest idea is the **Support Platform for PMOS and Endometriosis**. It addresses a real need for women seeking help with their health issues, and there are no clear competitors yet.

## Buildable Ideas

### Support Platform for PMOS and Endometriosis
**Category:** Women's health  
**Problem:** Women with PMOS and endometriosis struggle to find effective treatment and support.  
**What's already out there:** No clear competitor yet.  
**Who'd pay:** Women with PMOS and endometriosis seeking treatment and support.  
**How you'd win:** A dedicated platform offering tailored resources and community support specifically for PMOS and endometriosis.  
**First build:** Build a simple online community platform for sharing experiences and accessing resources.  
**Niche test:** Women with PMOS and endometriosis, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is unsexy operational work, boring score 5/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium risk; big companies like AbbVie or Pfizer could develop similar features.  
**Confidence:** Medium; a big company could easily enter this space.
