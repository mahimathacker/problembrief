## TL;DR
Today, there are a few buildable ideas, but they all have medium confidence. The strongest idea is about helping eBay sellers appeal bans. Many sellers are frustrated with the current process, and a dedicated service could make it easier for them.

## Buildable Ideas

### **eBay Ban Appeal Support**
**Category:** Consumer  
**Problem:** Users are frustrated with being permanently banned from eBay without clear reasons or a way to appeal.  
**What's already out there:** Amazon offers clearer ban policies, Etsy has more transparent appeal processes.  
**Who'd pay:** eBay sellers who have been banned or are at risk of being banned.  
**How you'd win:** A dedicated service that helps users understand the reasons for their bans and provides a structured appeal process.  
**First build:** Create a simple web app that guides users through the appeal process and offers templates for communication with eBay support.  
**Niche test:** eBay sellers facing bans, niche score 4/5.  
**Boring test:** This is operational work but focused on a specific problem, boring score 4/5.  
**Big-company risk:** Medium risk; eBay could enhance their customer support and appeal processes.  
**Confidence:** Medium; eBay could make this service less necessary.  
**Sources:** [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48832668)
