## Opportunities

### Accidental Approvals in Terminal Prompts

**The Problem:** Developers accidentally approve terminal prompts because of a poorly implemented clickable Yes/No feature, lacking an option to disable it. This leads to frequent disruptions and inefficiencies.  

**Why Now:** As command-line interfaces evolve, user experience improvements must balance automation and manual control to prevent costly mistakes.  

**Who'd Pay:** DevOps teams and developers who frequently rely on terminal operations would invest in a solution offering more control and precision.  

**Buildability Read:** Implementing a toggle or user-defined settings is straightforward but requires thorough testing to cover diverse use cases.

### Unexpected High Cloud Bills

**The Problem:** A mistake in configuring cloud services can lead to disastrous billing surprises, impacting especially small businesses or individual developers.  

**Why Now:** With increasing reliance on cloud services, transparent and manageable cost control tools are crucial to prevent financial blowouts due to human error.  

**Who'd Pay:** SMBs, startups, and freelancers eager to keep their cloud budgets under control and avoid rogue expenses would find value in such a solution.  

**Buildability Read:** Adding an intelligent monitoring feature that prompts users about unusual usage patterns could prevent such issues effectively.
